## Observed failure family

Repeated real artifact work showed six coupled failure forms: non-evidentiary
decisions ossified as freezes; local patches accumulated into global residue;
several validations shared one historical context; broad edits resurrected
previously removed defect classes; fresh readers saw abnormal structure that
historical reviewers rationalized; and exact counts displaced the true
artifact objective. The source episode was manuscript finalization, but the
mechanism is also present in software and benchmark release scenarios.

## Strongest no-change argument

P22 replanning can challenge stale assumptions; P7 and Add-Then-Reduce can
remove excess patches; P18 and negative knowledge can prevent recurrence;
P26 and M-self-audit can simulate a fresh reader; critical-thinking can
challenge proxy objectives. Downstream agents failed to apply those adequate
general mechanisms, so another rule could duplicate doctrine and add ceremony.

This argument is correct about primitive sufficiency and rejects a new P-n,
M-rule, K-state, core trigger, or mandatory reviewer system. It does not explain
how a fresh agent reaches the required operations specifically at final
promotion, in what order, what information must initially be withheld, which
freezes remain protected, or when to stop. Those missing activation and
ordering properties survived the no-change counterfactual.

  summary, benchmark fixtures, and strict verification scripts.

## Exact missing behavior

SUA had no bounded, reachable final-promotion sequence that jointly required:

1. semantic classification of frozen clauses before challenging them;
2. preservation of evidence/scientific invariants against post-hoc rescue;
3. objective recovery before optimizing exact measurable proxies;
4. removal-first reconsideration of accumulated implementation controls;
5. regression of stable killed-defect classes after broad mutations;
6. blind-first observation before historical rationales and PASS reports; and
7. a material-mutation retrigger plus one-audit stop condition.

Each primitive existed separately; the missing contract was their finalization
orchestration, evidence boundary, and discoverable activation point.

## Selected minimal solution

The retained mutation is [Artifact Finalization](ARTIFACT_FINALIZATION.md), a
project-layer adapter reached from the existing acceptance path and ordinary
reading graph. It adds no cognitive primitive or decision state. Independence
is implemented by information control; a separate agent is optional. Stable
defect classes use the artifact's existing contract, tests, or decision record.
The gate runs only on concrete risk signals and stops after one reconciled
blind-first audit unless a major artifact mutation or material finding occurs.

## Rejected heavier alternatives

- No new P-n or M-rule: existing principles express the required reasoning.
- No core-layer edit or hook: semantic classification cannot be safely forced
  by a string-matching commit gate.
- No mandatory multi-agent framework: shared history, not agent count alone,
  caused the correlation.
- No new universal negative ledger: only stable recurring defect classes are
  retained in an existing artifact-local surface.
- No fixed patch-count threshold: activation uses observed accumulation and
  promotion risk.
- No manuscript-specific template or layout rule.

## Remaining uncertainty

A live agent may still misclassify a mixed evidence/presentation freeze,
perform a shallow fresh audit, or reconstruct history from the artifact itself.
Blinded multi-model or human trials are needed to measure decision quality,
false-positive challenges, and whether information control materially reduces
correlated acceptance errors.
